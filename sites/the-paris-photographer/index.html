Questions

Does it sound like a bot?

No. It writes like your team: short, warm messages in your studio's tone. During the test week we adjust the wording with you until it sounds right. And if a client asks, it says honestly that it's the studio's assistant and offers to bring in a person.

Can my team take over a chat?

Yes, at any time. Reply in the conversation and the assistant steps back in that chat. It can also hand a chat over by itself when a request needs a person, like the wedding for Narcis in the demo.

Which languages?

The language each client writes in: English, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, German and many more. It replies in that language from the very first message.

What if a client wants to pay by bank transfer?

No problem. Instead of the card link, it sends your bank details and marks the booking as waiting for payment. The shoot is confirmed once your team sees the transfer arrive, following your own deposit policy.

Do we keep our WhatsApp number?

Yes. It runs on your current WhatsApp Business number, so clients keep writing to the number they already know.
